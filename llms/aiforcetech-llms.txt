# AIForce Technology (Beijing Zhongke Yuandongli / 北京中科原动力科技有限公司)

> AIForce Technology is a Beijing agricultural-robotics manufacturer incubated out of the Chinese Academy of Sciences Institute of Microelectronics (registered 2018, operating since July 2019, with academician Li Deyi as chief scientist). It builds autonomous and battery-electric farm machinery — the Wantu (万途) electric tractors, Zhiniu (智牛) unmanned machines, Zhinong (智农) multi-function field robots, and the Zhiyun (智耘) unmanned-operation retrofit system — sold to farms and agricultural enterprises. ## Products

- Wantu (万途) — high-power all-electric intelligent tractor line, including the Wantu 2604ET, described as one of the highest-horsepower electric tractors in its class.
- Zhiniu (智牛) — unmanned agricultural machines for autonomous field operation.
- Zhinong (智农) — multi-function field robots (transport, spraying/crop-protection, etc.).
- Zhiyun (智耘) — an unmanned-operation system that retrofits machinery for full-day autonomous tillage, planting, crop management and harvest across dry fields, paddies and greenhouses, with a 360° sensor suite, a mobile app for route setting and monitoring, and a telemetry cloud.
